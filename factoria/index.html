⚙️ Factoria Codex: Guide & Math Mechanics

1. The Objective

Construct mathematical equations using your hand of Number and Operator cards to reach or surpass each Vault's Target Score before running out of Hands.

2. Hand Scoring Formula

Hand Score = (Equation Result + Flat Chips) × (Base Mult + Bonus Mult) × X-Mult

The resulting value of your equation forms your starting Base Chips. Then, each played card and active Engine Module adds Chips, Multipliers, or multiplicative X-Mult.

3. Engine Modules (Relics)

You can hold up to 5 Engine Modules. They trigger from left to right on every valid hand! Build synergies such as:

  • Even-Steven: Multiplies even numbers with +4 Mult.
  • Clean Division: Exact division with 0 remainder multiplies total score by ×2.5!
  • Fibonacci Engine: Hitting Fibonacci totals (8, 13, 21, 34, 55, 89...) triples your score!

4. Card Types & Enhancements

  • Numbers (1-20+): Provide base value. Primes, Evens, and Squares activate specific relics.
  • Operators (+, -, ×, ÷, ^): Standard arithmetic. Valid equations must alternate correctly (e.g. [5] [×] [4] [+] [2] = 22).
  • Card Foils: Foil (+50 Chips), Holo (+10 Mult), Polychrome (×1.5 Mult).
