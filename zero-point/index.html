How to Play

🌟

1. Tiered Supernova Detonations

Opposites annihilate to 0! Larger numbers unleash exponentially bigger blasts:
• ±1: Micro pop
• ±2: ⚡ Pulse Shockwave
• ±4: ⚡ Cross Laser (blasts full row & column!)
• ±8+: 🌟 Supernova (obliterates 3×3 crater!)

+4

2. Same-Sign Synthesis (Craft Supernovas!)

Touching identical tiles fuse together (+2 & +2 = +4, +4 & +4 = +8). Build higher numbers to unleash screen-clearing Cross Lasers and Supernovas!

⚡

3. Singularity Overdrive & Frenzy

Build your energy meter to 100% with drops, syntheses, and Zero Point detonations. Press [F] or tap the glowing button to unleash Singularity Frenzy (3 rounds of devastating super-weapons: ⚫ Vortex column crush, 🌟 Nova Core, and 💥 Antimatter Nuke)!

±

4. Quantum Special Orbs

Watch for special drops: [±] Invert flips signs, [÷2] Fission halves a number, [×2] Amplify doubles it, and [0] Singularity clears surrounding areas!

⚡

5. Quantum Swap Tokens & Tactical Bounties

Earn Swap Tokens by completing in-run bounties, cascades, or drops. Press [S] (or tap Swap) to swap any 2 adjacent board tiles to ignite monster Supernovas or rescue a tight situation!

#

6. Watch the Ceiling!

Every 5 drops, a new row of dormant Dark Matter rises from the bottom. Shatter them with Zero Point blasts. Don't let blocks cross the red danger line!
